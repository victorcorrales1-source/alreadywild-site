For creators

We work with independent authors and illustrators from end to end: finding good work, refining it until it is ready to publish, then marketing and selling it.

What we offer:

  • Launch kits for finished picture books: your listing written and ready, back-cover copy, an advance-reader page set up in your name, a booked promotion and a six-week launch calendar.
  • Manuscript editing and review: an audit and a tracked edit of your text, so you accept or reject every change.
  • Full publishing, by invitation: from finished manuscript to a marketed, distributed book, run as a partnership. We read every submission and only take on books we believe in. It is not open yet; tell us you are interested and we will write to you first.

A person reviews every kit. We keep your manuscript confidential and you keep every right to your book. We use AI tools to help with drafting, and a human checks the work. Details are shared when each offer opens.
