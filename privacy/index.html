Privacy policy.

Last updated November 20, 2026.

What we collect

If you join an email list on this site (for example Eye of Eden cards or KnightQuest lessons), we collect your email address. That is all the form asks for. Our email service, EmailOctopus, also records basic list activity such as when you subscribed and whether you opened or clicked an email.

Downloading a free PDF needs no sign-up, and we do not ask for your name, age or address.

Why

We use your email only to send you the cards, lessons and Already Wild updates you signed up for. We don't sell or share your email address.

Who handles it

EmailOctopus stores the list and sends our emails for us, as our service provider. The sign-up form is protected by Google reCAPTCHA, which is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Children

Our email lists are for adults 18 and over. Please do not enter a child's email address or information. If a child's address ends up on a list, write to us and we will remove it.

Unsubscribe or ask us to delete your email

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and it works in one click. You can also write to hello@alreadywild.com and we will remove your address and anything we hold about you.
